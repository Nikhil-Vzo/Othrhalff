## Overview

This guide walks you through setting up a **100% Free Forever** business email on your domain (`othrhalff.in`) using **Zoho Mail**, followed by DNS configuration (SPF, DKIM, DMARC) so your pitches land directly in **Gmail Primary Inboxes** instead of Spam.

## Step 2: Add DNS Records in Your Domain Provider

Log in to wherever your domain DNS is managed (e.g., **Cloudflare**, **Hostinger**, **GoDaddy**, **Namecheap**, or **BigRock**).

Navigate to **DNS Management / DNS Records** and add the following:

### 1. Domain Ownership Verification (TXT Record)
*Zoho will show you a unique code in your setup wizard:*
* **Type:** `TXT`
* **Name / Host:** `@` (or leave blank)
* **Value / Content:** `zoho-verification=zbXXXXXXXX.zoho.in`
* **TTL:** `Auto` or `3600`

---

### 2. MX Records (Required to Receive Incoming Emails)
*Add these 3 records to route incoming mail to Zoho:*

| Type | Name / Host | Mail Server / Value | Priority | TTL |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **MX** | `@` | `mx.zoho.in` | **10** | Auto / 3600 |
| **MX** | `@` | `mx2.zoho.in` | **20** | Auto / 3600 |
| **MX** | `@` | `mx3.zoho.in` | **50** | Auto / 3600 |

*(Note: If you already have old default MX records from your host, delete them so there are no conflicts).*

---

### 3. SPF Record (Required to Prevent Emails Going to Spam)
*Tells Gmail that Zoho is authorized to send emails on behalf of `othrhalff.in`:*

* **Type:** `TXT`
* **Name / Host:** `@`
* **Value / Content:** `v=spf1 include:zoho.in ~all`
* **TTL:** `Auto` or `3600`

---

### 4. DKIM Record (Cryptographic Signature Verification)
*Inside your Zoho Admin Panel → Email Authentication → DKIM:*
1. Click **Add Selector** (e.g., selector name: `zoho`).
2. Zoho will generate a long TXT value for your domain.
3. In your DNS settings, add:
   * **Type:** `TXT`
   * **Name / Host:** `zoho._domainkey` (or `zoho._domainkey.othrhalff.in`)
   * **Value / Content:** `v=DKIM1; k=rsa; p=MIGfMA0GCSqGSIb3...` *(Paste the exact key generated by Zoho)*
   * **TTL:** `Auto` or `3600`
4. Go back to Zoho and click **Verify / Enable DKIM**.

---

### 5. DMARC Record (Domain Reputation Protection)
*Signals to Gmail and Outlook that your domain is strictly protected against spoofing:*

* **Type:** `TXT`
* **Name / Host:** `_dmarc` (or `_dmarc.othrhalff.in`)
* **Value / Content:** `v=DMARC1; p=none; rua=mailto:othrhalff@gmail.com`
* **TTL:** `Auto` or `3600`

## Step 6: The 5 Anti-Spam Golden Rules for Investor Pitches

1. **Address the Recipient Personally:** Always start with `"Hi [First Name],"` rather than `"Dear Sir/Madam"` or `"To whom it may concern"`.
2. **Never Send Mass BCCs:** Always send emails individually (1 recipient per email).
3. **Keep Links Minimal:** Maximum 2 clean links per email (e.g., your website `https://www.othrhalff.in` and your Pitch Deck Google Drive / DocSend link).
4. **No Attachments over 5MB:** Prefer sharing a clean link to your pitch deck rather than attaching a 20MB file that might trigger email security filters.
5. **Include a Professional Email Signature:**

```text
--
Nikhil V.
Founder, Othrhalff
Website: https://www.othrhalff.in
Raipur / Bhilai, Chhattisgarh | +91 [Your Phone Number]
LinkedIn: [Your LinkedIn URL]
```
