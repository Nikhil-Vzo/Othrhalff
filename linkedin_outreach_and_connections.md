## Rules of LinkedIn Outreach

1. **Character Limit:** Free LinkedIn connection requests have a strict **300-character limit**. All notes below are pre-counted to be under 290 characters.
2. **Daily Volume:** Send **maximum 15–20 connection requests per day** to keep your LinkedIn account safe and avoid automated flags.
3. **The 2-Step Flow:**
   * **Step 1:** Send the customized connection request note.
   * **Step 2:** Once accepted, wait 2–4 hours and send the **First Follow-Up DM**.
