# Part 1: The Cold Outreach Playbook & Exact Copy Templates

> **Core Principle:** When asking for money, you often get advice. When asking for advice and demonstrating sharp execution, you often get connections and funding.

---

### Template A: Founder-to-Founder / Mentor Connection (Advice & Insights)
*Use this when reaching out to experienced product builders, consumer founders, or community leaders. The goal is to build a relationship, not immediately ask for a cheque.*

```text
Subject: Quick question on campus viral loops & retention for student social app (from Bhilai/Raipur)

Hi [Name],

I’ve been following your journey with [Their Company/Project]—especially how you handled early user acquisition and community density.

I’m Nikhil, building Othrhalff (https://www.othrhalff.in), a real-time, low-friction campus social and anonymous confession/chat platform for college students. We are seeing early traction across engineering campuses in Chhattisgarh and preparing our multi-campus rollout.

I'm currently solving for our D7 retention curve and campus ambassador incentives. Given your experience in consumer apps, would you have 10 minutes for a brief chat or even 2 bullet points of advice over email?

Either way, appreciate the inspiring work you're putting out!

Best regards,
Nikhil V.
Founder, Othrhalff (https://www.othrhalff.in)
Raipur / Bhilai, Chhattisgarh | +91 [Your Phone Number]
LinkedIn: [Your LinkedIn Profile Link]
```

### Template B: Pre-Seed Angel / VC Pitch (Traction & Funding Ask)
*Use this for angel investors, micro-VCs, and seed funds. Keep it crisp, metrics-driven, and easy to read on mobile.*

```text
Subject: Pre-Seed Pitch: Othrhalff - Real-Time Campus Social & Connection Network for College Students

Hi [Investor Name],

I am reaching out to introduce Othrhalff (https://www.othrhalff.in), a modern, anonymous-first social discovery and real-time interaction platform built specifically for college campuses.

Why Othrhalff?
Traditional social platforms suffer from high social friction and performative posting. Othrhalff brings back authentic peer interactions via instant low-latency text/video chats, verified campus channels, and student confessions.

Key Metrics & Highlights:
- Live Platform: Active production app at https://www.othrhalff.in with WebRTC instant speed chat and campus directory.
- Target Market: 40M+ Indian higher-education students seeking authentic campus discovery and peer connections.
- Core Moat: Hyper-local campus density, zero-friction onboarding, and viral college confession loops.
- Founders: Engineering-led team based out of Raipur/Bhilai with strong product architecture & local campus distribution.

We are raising our pre-seed angel round of [₹25L – ₹50L / $50k] to expand to 20+ engineering and university campuses across Central & South India.

Would you be open to reviewing our 1-page deck or taking a brief 15-min intro call next week?

Pitch Deck Link: [Insert Google Drive / DocSend Link]

Best regards,
Nikhil V.
Founder, Othrhalff
+91 [Your Phone Number] | Raipur / Bhilai
Website: https://www.othrhalff.in
```
